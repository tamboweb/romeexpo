Put your artifact photos here, e.g. coin.jpg, helmet.jpg, vase.jpg, temple.jpg

Tips:
- JPG or PNG both work.
- A square-ish photo (or close to it) looks best - the exhibit shrinks it to fit either way.
- Keep file sizes small (a phone photo resized to around 800px wide is plenty) so the page loads fast on the Pi.
- The filename here must exactly match the "image" value you put in config/artifacts.json.
